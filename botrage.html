What it is

A field guide, not a landing page

botrage.me is the front door for the whole project: serious enough to show people what exists, weird enough to still be ours. It is organised as a field guide in three chapters — the software that ships, the agents on duty, and the entries archived with honours — so a product, a bot and a memorial share one grammar instead of three different websites.

It is also a useful artifact by itself: plain files, no tracking, no build step, quick to load and easy to read in view-source, backed by a normal GitHub repository instead of a mystery pile of screenshots and vibes.

See alsoChapter III — 00 Illidan, 0∞ Fenix, ✦ The Fun Forge. Kept, not deleted.

The archive chapter is the part that matters most. Nothing here is deleted when it stops running — an agent that is switched off keeps its entry, its dates and its record of service, because in this fleet archived is not death: files on storage can always be read again.
